One-line: Empty or unconfigured state — graphite icon glyph, one line of explanation, at most one action.

```jsx
<EmptyState icon="settings_input_antenna" title="No units in this region"
  action={<Button variant="secondary" size="sm">Clear filters</Button>}>
  Units appear here once they check in from the field.
</EmptyState>
```

Keep it quiet: no amber, no illustration, no oversized icon.
