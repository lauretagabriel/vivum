One-line: Light counterpart to the navy canvas — Ice Blue paper for documents, print, and light product UI.

```jsx
<Sheet pad={64}>
  <h2 style={{ color: 'var(--text-heading-on-light)' }}>Field report</h2>
  <p>…</p>
</Sheet>
```

Inside a Sheet, headings are Deep Navy, body is Slate, captions stay Graphite, and buttons switch to `variant="onLight"`.
